This is a line chart, with each quarter of visits on the x-axis and the percentage of follow ups met on the y-axis. There are two lines, one for the Home-based program and one for the Center-based program. This line chart shows that home-based follow up rate is lower than the center-based follow up rate, with the largest gap between the two being at the final quarter [70% for home-based vs 79% for center-based]. A limitation for this chart is that the patients were able to freely choose their program so the groups may be skewed due to possible confounders.
